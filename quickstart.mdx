## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info">

Before starting, ensure you have:
- A Google Account (free to create at accounts.google.com)
- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari

</Callout>

## Sign In with Google

Follow these steps to access Bryce Arce and create your first documentation space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit Bryce Arce" icon="globe">
    Open your browser and navigate to `https://brycearce.com`.

    Click the **Sign In** button in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Google Sign-In" icon="log-in">
    Select **Sign in with Google** from the authentication options.

    Grant permissions for Bryce Arce to access your Google Account for secure, one-click access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Setup" icon="check-circle">
    After redirection, you'll land on the dashboard.

    Bryce Arce uses your Google Account for simple, secure organization of your docs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create Your First Doc Space

Organize projects by creating dedicated documentation spaces.

<Steps>
  <Step title="New Doc Space" icon="plus">
    From the dashboard, click **New Space**.

    Enter a name like `My Project Docs` and select a color (e.g., brand orange `#ef7025`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your First Document" icon="file-text">
    Inside the space, select **New Document**.

    Choose a template or start blank. Title it `Quickstart Notes`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
