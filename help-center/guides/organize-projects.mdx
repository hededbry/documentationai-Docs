## Organize Your Docs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new project folder">
    Click "New Folder" in your dashboard and name it for your project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add documentation">
    Open the folder and click "New Doc" to start writing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite collaborators">
    Share the folder with teammates using their Google email.
  </Step>
</Steps>
