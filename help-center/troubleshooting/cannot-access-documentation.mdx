---
title: Cannot Access Documentation
description: Help if you can't access Bryce Arce docs.
---

## Symptoms

- Login page keeps looping
- "Access denied" message

## Causes

- Not signed in with Google
- Incorrect permissions

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Click "Sign in with Google" on the homepage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check permissions">
    Ask the doc owner to grant you access via your Google email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    ````bash
    # Press Shift+F5 or clear cache in browser settings
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
